What goes red if a dangerous path in the diff breaks:

- Enter, Tab or Escape writing twice, or Escape writing: rows 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6 count every PATCH, POST and DELETE on the wire.
- An incomplete date or time writing on Enter, Tab or leave: rows 1, 2 and 19.
- A portalled click ending the edit through the cell, or writing twice: rows 1, 2, 4, 5 and 6.
- A popover going light in dark mode: row 13 and every editor row's light and dark pass, by measured luminance.
- A popover covering its cell: the select, to-one and to-many rows assert no overlap; row 14 catches the calendar.
- Not covered by any browser test: the time editor and the sheet's long-text editor (rows 3 and 10), which only the unit suite reaches.
